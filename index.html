Notes & method

  • Source. The Weekly Social Analytics leadership emails covering . Only the Crooked Ideas section of each email is used; scripts/parse_emails.py reads the emails and writes data/crooked-ideas.json.
  • Totals are as reported. The account-total charts plot each email's TOTALS row rather than a sum of its platform rows, which is how the numbers in the weekly deck were produced.
  • Where the rows disagree. Platform rows and the TOTALS row are hand-assembled and rarely agree to the digit. Weeks where they differ by more than 5% on any metric are flagged in the tooltip and greyed in the table. The week of 7/13–7/19 is the notable one: that email repeats the previous week's Instagram and TikTok figures, so its platform split is unreliable even though its totals are not.
  • Placeholder weeks. A week with no email yet can be hand-entered in data/placeholder-weeks.json. It is drawn with a dashed line and hollow markers, labelled in the table, and marked placeholder in the CSV; any metric not supplied stays blank rather than being guessed at. When the real email arrives it replaces the placeholder automatically.
  • Threads. Followers are reported; impressions and engagements are not, so Threads appears only in the follower charts.
  • Engagement rate is the rate printed in the email, not a recomputed ratio — except on placeholder weeks, where no email printed one and it is computed as engagements ÷ impressions.
  • Raw data: crooked-ideas.json. Rebuild with python3 scripts/parse_emails.py <dir-of-emails>.
